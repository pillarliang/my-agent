Quality: Observability & Evaluation

1. Architectural Upgrade: Observability and Evaluation are Now Independent Modules

In Agent system architecture, Observability and Evaluation serve completely different lifecycles and objectives:

  • Observability answers "What actually happened during runtime?": Online distributed tracing, token consumption auditing, latency profiling, and error root-cause analysis.
  • Evaluation answers "Did the task deliverable truly meet the quality bar?": Offline benchmarks, isolated sandboxes, deterministic state diff assertions, and multi-tiered scoring.

To provide deeper, cleaner explanations, the Quality chapter has been decoupled into two dedicated standalone modules:

👉 Observability: Runtime Telemetry & Tracing

Detailed coverage of Trace, Generation, Span, and Score primitives, pairing with Langfuse for call-tree visualization and non-blocking telemetry.

👉 Evaluation: Agent Benchmarks & Sandboxes

Comprehensive guide to Agent evaluation paradigms, incorporating Inspect AI, DeepEval, and SWE-bench for sandbox isolation, deterministic state verification, and metrics.
