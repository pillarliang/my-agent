On-Demand Capability Guide: Extensions, Multi-Entry Interfaces & Multi-Agent Orchestration

When a local CLI and fixed built-in tools are no longer enough, consult the following modules and stages based on your scenario:

  • Practical evolution path: See Stage 7: Add Entry Points, Extensions & Orchestration on Demand.
  • Multiple user entry points (Web / CLI sharing a service): See Interfaces: Share Execution Flow Across Entry Points.
  • Capability extensions (Skills, Hooks, Middleware, MCP): See Extensions: Connect Skills, Hooks & External Tools.
  • Multi-agent division of labor & convergence (Subagent, Handoff, Graph): See Orchestration: Coordinate Multi-Agent Division of Labor.
